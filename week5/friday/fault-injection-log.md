## What the faults showed beyond the expected

- **Rows 3 and 4 together prove the parallel Verify stage.** In each case the *other* branch finished and passed, so the failure was isolated to one check.
- **Row 6 was not staged.** The SCM poller rebuilt an already-published commit on its own, twice. It exposed a real tension between "re-running a build is harmless" and "a version is immutable" (see `reflection.md`).
